Research

My research explores how reinforcement learning can learn reusable representations for decision-making, clustering, and classification. My interests also include hierarchical reinforcement learning, imitation learning, generative AI, and natural language processing.

Research experience and projects

  • MDP autoencoder: behaviorally grounded, concise representations of decision-making problems with high-dimensional observations.
  • Imitation learning: mapping human demonstrations into robot space to enable robots to perform new tasks.
  • Adaptive neural network classifiers: incrementally constructing classifiers for changing tasks, large datasets, and streaming data.
  • Structured representation learning: using policy gradient algorithms to enforce structural constraints on neural network features.
  • Semi-unsupervised clustering: learning representations for context-aware clustering.
  • Master’s project: a multi-agent path finder using ant colony and bee colony optimization to locate, track, and plan paths to static or dynamic targets.
