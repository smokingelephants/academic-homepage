Research

My research explores learning representations for decision-making: behaviorally grounded latent models, adaptive neural network classifiers, and learning structured representations with reinforcement learning.

My doctoral dissertation at the University of Texas at Arlington was Learning Representations Using Reinforcement Learning (2019).
